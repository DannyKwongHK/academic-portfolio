Qualitative research

Welfare deservingness

How do people decide who deserves welfare support? This work examines public judgements of deservingness and welfare legitimacy in Hong Kong, including the context of the COVID-19 pandemic.

My responsibilities include conducting focus groups, transcription, qualitative coding, and drafting manuscript findings and discussion sections.

MPhil research · 2022–2024

Inside competitive
yo-yoing.

An ethnographic study of group dynamics, gender, and field in Hong Kong’s yo-yoing sport subculture.

My MPhil research at Lingnan University was supervised by Professor Ruby Lai and Professor Chen Hon Fai. This line of work also informs my manuscript on group hierarchy and contestant identity in competitive yo-yoing.
